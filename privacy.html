Privacy

India Helpline collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no location permission request.

Location is chosen by you, manually, from a list. What you search for stays in your browser — it is never sent to a server, put in a URL, or logged.

The site makes no requests to any third-party server. Fonts, icons and data are bundled with the site itself.

If you save the directory for offline use, it is stored in your browser's cache on your own device, with its saved date shown on the site.

Reporting incorrect details sends only what you type, to the site's contact channel — it is not a way to reach emergency services.

India Helpline is an independent public-service directory. It lists verified phone numbers only — it does not receive emergency requests and never places calls automatically. For any emergency, call 112.
