Women Helpline 181 — Mission Shakti

Toll-free 24-hour service for any woman in distress or facing violence, or someone calling on her behalf. The operator connects her to ERSS (112) for police, fire or ambulance and to One Stop Centres for legal support and counselling. Not operational in West Bengal — there, call 112 or the NCW helpline.
