Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 — financial fraud

Report online and financial frauds (UPI, net-banking and similar). Prompt calls feed a system linked with banks so fraudulent transactions can be frozen. Full complaints can also be filed on cybercrime.gov.in, which has an anonymous section for women and children.
